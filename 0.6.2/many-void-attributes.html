Excessive void attributes

Objects must have at most 5 void attributes. The receiver ^ is not counted, even when declared explicitly.

Incorrect:

[a b c d e f] > with-many
  something > @

Correct:

[a b c d e] > with-ok
  something > @

Also correct, since ^ is the receiver, not an argument:

[^ a b c d e] > with-receiver
  something > @
