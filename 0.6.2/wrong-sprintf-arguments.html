Wrong .printf Arguments

The .printf object must have matching placeholder variables and passed arguments.

Incorrect:

[name] > app
  io.stdout > @
    "Hello, %s! Your account is %d.".printf
      * name

Correct:

[name acc] > app
  io.stdout > @
    "Hello, %s! Your account is %d.".printf
      * name acc
