Empty alias

Object aliases must not be empty. The EO parser already rejects a bare +alias, so this defect shows up in XMIR produced by other tools.

Incorrect:

<object>
  <metas>
    <meta>
      <head>alias</head>
      <tail/>
    </meta>
  </metas>
  <o name="foo"/>
</object>

Correct:

# Foo.

+alias a
+alias b c
+alias d e f
+alias g h i j

[] > foo
