Comment Starts With Article

Comments should not start with an article, such as a, an, or the. An article at the beginning of a comment adds no information and makes the documentation more verbose than necessary.

Incorrect:

# The object that calculates the sum.
[] > foo
  42 > @

Correct:

# Object that calculates the sum.
[] > foo
  42 > @

The check is case-insensitive, so A, An, and The are flagged as well. Words that merely begin with the same letters as an article, such as Another or Theory, are not affected.
