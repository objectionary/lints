Unsorted metas

Metas must be alphabetically ordered.

Incorrect:

+alias org.eolang.io.stdout
+alias org.eolang.io.stdin

[] > foo

Correct:

+alias org.eolang.io.stdin
+alias org.eolang.io.stdout

[] > foo

In the defect message, the meta is quoted with every space replaced by ⌴ (U+2314): The "alias⌴stdout⌴org.eolang.io.stdout" meta is out of order. This is not a stray character. Every lint marks spaces this way in quoted text, so a leading, trailing, or repeated space is never mistaken for a printing artifact.
