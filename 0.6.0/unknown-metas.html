Unknown metas

The following metas are supported:

  • +package
  • +alias
  • +also
  • +version
  • +rt
  • +architect
  • +home
  • +unlint
  • +probe
  • +syntax

Incorrect:

+foo
+bar

[] > foo

Correct:

+package com.test
+alias foo
+also foo
+version 0.0.0
+architect yegor256@gmail.com
+rt jvm
+home https://earth.com
+unlint unsorted-metas
+syntax 0.1.0

[] > foo
